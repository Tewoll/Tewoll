## 💫 About Me

I'm a Software Engineer from Palembang, Indonesia, with 3+ years of experience building web-based information systems and ERP workflows for construction project management.

My main focus is PHP, Laravel, JavaScript, MySQL, Git, and GitHub. I enjoy turning operational requirements into practical, maintainable, and useful software solutions.

My experience includes developing ERP workflows related to BoQ, RAB, budget allocation, cost monitoring, company portfolio websites, and SEO optimization.

Currently, I am also exploring practical AI integrations, including a personal Telegram AI assistant.

- 🎯 Focus: Web applications, ERP systems, and construction technology
- 💬 Ask me about: PHP, Laravel, JavaScript, MySQL, Git, and web-based information systems
- 📍 Location: Palembang, Indonesia
- 🎓 Education: Bachelor of Information Systems, Universitas Bina Darma
